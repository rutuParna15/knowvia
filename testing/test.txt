Dear Madam,
We are pleased to inform you that a team of final-year students from your esteemed institution, pursuing B.E. in Artificial Intelligence and Machine Learning, is actively engaged in an industry-oriented R&D initiative with Vektor3D Systems LLP. The project, titled “Automated Anomaly Detection in 3D Printing”, focuses on developing an AI-powered computer vision pipeline for real-time defect detection in additive manufacturing processes.
The system leverages convolutional neural networks, transfer learning on pre-trained deep models, and real-time multi-frame image analytics to detect micro-level deviations during the printing process. The solution aims to address challenges such as print failure mitigation, predictive quality assurance, and adaptive feedback control in 3D printing workflows.
